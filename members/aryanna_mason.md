## My Issues and Contributions 

I worked primarily on the Archetypes & Methods section of the project. I focused especially on the Explorer, Innocent, and Hero archetypes. I researched their characteristics, design styles, persuasion methods, color schemes, imagery, and examples, and used that information to develop the archetype pages.

I also created and organized images that represented each archetype and showed how different design styles and persuasion methods could be used together. I worked on creating examples for the archetypes using different design movements, including Modernism and Postmodernism, and connected them to appropriate persuasion principles.

- **Archetypes & Methods of Persuasion** — Matched the **Explorer, Innocent, and Hero** archetypes with appropriate **methods of persuasion**. I explained why each method matched the archetype, added **real-world examples and original examples**, and created **AI-generated images** that represented the archetype and persuasion concepts.
